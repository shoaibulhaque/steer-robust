skills from Steer Robust.

"hidden links", "needless links", "surplus", and "special cases" from the
[`untangle-codebase` skill](https://github.com/caldoai/skills/blob/main/skills/untangle-codebase/SKILL.md)

retained in `LICENSE.txt` for standalone installation.

refactoring mandate to task-scoped design evaluation, and adds greenfield, brownfield, migration,
tradeoff evaluation and compatibility guidance. This does not imply author endorsement or that
agent-behavior improvements have been measured.
